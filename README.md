# Coffee-Shop-Analysis

Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the BrightLearn Data Analytics Case Study. The objective of the projective was to obtain insights from historical sales data to understand product performance, identify peak and slow sales periods, and uncover opportunities to improve revenue.

The analysis focuses on:

Understanding sales patterns across product categories and time intervals.
Identifying peak sales periods during the day.
Identifying products that generate the highest revenue.
Providing data-driven information aimed at improving business performance.
Business Problem Bright Coffee Shop has appointed a new CEO with a strategic objective to grow revenue and improve overall product performance. As a newly appointed data analyst, my task is to analyse historical transaction data to identify the key drivers of revenue and sales volume, determine peak and slow sales periods, evaluate product and category performance, and identify opportunities for targeted operational and marketing actions. The findings will provide the CEO with evidence-based insights to support decisions aimed at increasing revenue, improving product performance, and optimising sales activity.

The project aims to:

Analyse product performance by calculating total revenue.
Analyse product performance by calculating total units sold.
Analyse product performance by calculating and ranking highest selling products.
Analyse time performance by calculating which time periods have the highest revenue.
Analyse time performance by calculating which time periods have the units sold.
Analysis of category performance by calculating which product categories with highest revenue.
Analysis of category performance by calculating and ranking which product categories have the most units sold.
